#+LATEX: \newpage
* Community Vision
- e.g. space to share/gain insights spending time on the problem
- e.g. figuring best practices working with AI
- e.g. knowledge exchange, the super-intelligence in action

#+LATEX: \newpage
* Values/principles
- e.g. Intentionality - every event has a purpose for this community, at this moment. 

#+LATEX: \newpage
* Motivation/Why

- AI is coming. Let's get ready. Together
- We need to learn how to build AI-/agentic-systems - how augmentation looks like
- And/But we (also) need to learn how to use AI/agents to build these systems and more (we don't know what will happen)
- This meet up is about the later (not the former)

#+LATEX: \newpage
* Content/What

- 2025 was all about individuals getting better at performing certain tasks (using cursor, windsurf, claude, codex, ...)
- The working assumption was that the AI will augment the engineer (hence the name)
- But things are moving fast (and faster than I thought)
- In/For 2026 the adoption has moved on (needs to move on) from the individual/task level to the org/process level
- Means (by now) the name of the meetup is actually a little bit misleading (sooner or later we need to fix this)
- We want to year about tools/tooling, processes, best-practices (the good, bad and the ugly)

#+LATEX: \newpage
* Execution/How

- We are meeting every two month. 18:00 to 20:30 (not longer). We are doing two presentations (45 mins/each)
- The first presentation is always a guest speaker
- The second presentation is always Roland doing a no-slides/only-code session, because ...
  - This way the first presentation can run over and nobody (besides Roland) gets hurt
  - People have expressed an interest in more hands-on demos and less slides (and Roland knows how to do that)
  - Roland likes the sound of his own voice
- For every meetup we need a sponsor (location and pizza/beer for 50-80 people (EUR 600-1000))

#+LATEX: \newpage
* Experience/Goal

We want people to walk away from a/the meetup and say ...

- This was worth-while my time. I have learned something (good value)
- This was nice. I had a good-time (good experience)

... and that means, I will tell other people about it and I will come/attend again.
