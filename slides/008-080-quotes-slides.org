* The quote-the-quote filter

14 Apr. -- 14 Jul. 2026

- The person said it in the three-month window
- An independent author reproduced the words verbatim
- Paraphrases, summaries, aggregators, and self-quotation do not count
- Three qualifying quotations or the person leaves the list

#+BEGIN_NOTES

This edition applies a deliberately severe relevance test. A speaker's own post
proves that they said something; it does not prove that the statement travelled.
Every quotation shown here was selected and reproduced by somebody else.

An independent interview, edited transcript, news report, or quotation page can
qualify several excerpts when it actually prints the speaker's words. A page
that merely links to, paraphrases, or summarises the original does not qualify.

Six previous candidates were removed because fewer than three displayed lines
survived this exact-text test: Steve Yegge, Martin Fowler, Kent Beck, Simon
Willison, Addy Osmani, and Armin Ronacher. This is an evidence result, not a
judgement that their work is unimportant.

* 21 repeated quotes / 5 signals

#+REVEAL_HTML: <div style="font-size: 0.76em; text-align: left;">

1. *Code becomes abundant.* Intent and accepted outcomes become scarce.
1. *The bottleneck migrates.* From writing to review, verification, and judgement.
1. *The harness matters.* Context, tools, feedback, constraints, and loops compound capability.
1. *Systems thinking differentiates.* Generating code is not engineering a system.
1. *Responsibility remains human.* Faster generation raises the cost of weak discipline.

#+REVEAL_HTML: </div>

#+BEGIN_NOTES

The strict filter makes the convergence more credible. Torvalds is cautious;
Majors describes an economic inversion; Cherny and Lopopolo report from teams
pushing autonomy; Böckeler supplies a risk model; Huntley separates coding from
engineering; Karpathy preserves the professional quality bar.

Do not optimise for generated code, agent count, or token spend. Make intent
explicit, create hard feedback, constrain the execution environment, keep work
observable, and measure accepted outcomes. The human role moves upward and
outward, but responsibility does not disappear.
